Food and water.
Support that continues.

GoodRise Canada sponsors Omar and his siblings in The Gambia. Every month, we send support so they can buy food and water.

When your salon partners with GoodRise, we provide this ongoing support on your salon’s behalf. Your customers can follow that journey with you.

Support is part of the partnership, not a fixed donation attached to each appointment.
